# Extraction — DWE, Gesetz zur Überführung von Wohnimmobilien in Gemeineigentum (Vergesellschaftungsgesetz – VergG), Entwurf, Stand 26.09.2025

Source: `pdf/2025_09_26_dwe_vergesellschaftungsgesetz_2d336724db.pdf` → markdown (19 pages). The actual draft-law text published by "Deutsche Wohnen & Co. enteignen" on 26 September 2025 — a materially updated version of the draft law that was included in full inside `DWE-Hg_2022_Wie-Vergesellschaftung-gelingt.pdf`. This is the current version of DWE's own legislative proposal, not a secondary description of it.

**This is a significant find for the model's "bigger picture":** the compensation mechanism in the actual current draft law is structurally different from what the model represents (a simple Verkehrswert × Entschädigungsquote slider).

## Data

- **Scope:** Überführung von rund 220.000 Wohnungen (per PwC-Whitepaper's citation of this law, S. 3) — **not 240.000** as used everywhere else in this project's model and sources. Worth resolving: is 220,000 an updated, narrower scope (only companies still over the 3,000-unit threshold as of 2025) or a rounding/estimation difference? Not yet verified against the draft law's own text (the law defines scope by mechanism — §§ 1–2, 3,000-unit threshold — not by a headline number).
- **Compensation basis (§ 12 Ausmaß der Entschädigung, § 13 Vergesellschaftungswert):** Compensation is based on a **"Vergesellschaftungswert,"** determined via the **Sachwertverfahren** (asset/replacement-value method, §§ 14–18) — **not** the Ertragswertverfahren (income capitalization) that this project's model and its other sources (Gutachterausschuss, BBU-Sodan) treat as the legally applicable method for rented residential property. This is a real methodological divergence between DWE's own current legislative proposal and the model's framing, not just a compensation-quota difference.
- **Bodenwert mechanism (§ 16):** The land-value component is **not current market land value** — it's the arithmetic-mean Bodenrichtwert for 2011–2013, indexed forward from 1 January 2013 at a fixed **3,5 % per year** to the law's effective date (§ 16 Abs. 2, referencing § 193 Abs. 4 Bewertungsgesetz). Since Berlin land values have risen far faster than 3.5%/year since 2013, this mechanically produces a compensation figure well below current market value — this is very likely the actual legal basis for the "40–60 % of Verkehrswert" figure the PwC whitepaper cites (see below), rather than an arbitrary political quota.
- **Payment form (§ 12 area, Schuldverschreibungen provisions around line 466–504):** Compensation is paid not in cash but via **100-year Schuldverschreibungen (bonds)** issued by the Land Berlin, at a fixed interest rate of **3,5 % per year**, tradeable on a regulated market (§ "Zulassung der Schuldverschreibungen zum Handel am regulierten Markt").
- **Not found in the draft law text itself:** the headline "40–60 % of Verkehrswert" and "14,5–17,0 Mrd. €" total figures. These come from a companion **Factsheet** (`2025_09_26_dwe_vergesellschaftungsgesetz_fact_sheet_52d7d13b1a.pdf`, cited by the PwC whitepaper, footnote 2, URL: `https://content.dwenteignen.de/uploads/2025_09_26_dwe_vergesellschaftungsgesetz_fact_sheet_52d7d13b1a.pdf`) that is **not yet in this project's `pdf/` folder** — worth adding if the model is updated to reflect this newer draft law, since the percentage/total figures are the ones actually usable as model parameters.

## Relational / modeling relevance

- The model's "Faire-Mieten-Modell (DWE)" preset (≈14 Mrd. €, 3,70 €/m² Zielmiete, 100% purchaseFactor at a lowered effective price) is based on the 2022 DWE book. This 2025 draft law is DWE's current, legally concrete proposal and uses a different mechanism (formula-based Sachwertverfahren with a frozen/indexed Bodenwert, not a simple percentage-of-Verkehrswert) and a different headline total (14,5–17,0 Mrd. € vs. the model's ≈14 Mrd. €) and a different unit count (≈220,000 vs. 240,000).
- **Not yet reflected in the model or its presets** — this is a scope decision for Peer, not something to change unilaterally: (a) update the "Faire-Mieten-Modell" preset to the 2025 figures, (b) add a new fifth preset for the 2025 draft law alongside the existing four, or (c) leave the model as an intentionally simplified percentage-based illustration and just note in the background text that the actual current draft law uses a different, formula-based mechanism.
- The bond-based, 100-year, 3,5%-interest payment structure is not modeled at all currently (the model assumes either loan financing or Landeshaushalt equity, not tradeable long-dated compensation bonds held by the former owners) — a structurally different question from the state's financing cost that the current model computes.
